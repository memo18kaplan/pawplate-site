Toxic
🐱 For cats
Toxic
⚠️ If your pet ate something toxic, call your veterinarian or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center ((888) 426-4435) right away.
Why
Some wild mushrooms are highly poisonous and are hard to tell apart. Plain store-bought mushrooms are generally considered fine for dogs.
How to serve
Keep pets away from mushrooms growing outdoors.
