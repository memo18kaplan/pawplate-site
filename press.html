Press kit

An iPhone journal for owners of itchy dogs and cats: a 10-second daily check-in, a guided 8-week elimination diet and a one-page report for the vet.

In short

PawPlate helps dog and cat owners find out what makes their pet itch. A 10-second daily check-in turns into patterns and a one-page PDF a vet can read in 30 seconds. It guides an 8-week elimination diet, scans food labels for past triggers and rates 145 foods, plants and household items separately for dogs and cats, with sources. No account, no ads, no tracking.

About PawPlate

Vets usually check for food allergy in dogs and cats with an elimination diet: one food for about eight weeks, then old foods added back one at a time. The hard part is the record keeping. PawPlate makes it a 10-second daily habit: rate the itch from 0 to 10 on a scale modelled on the one vets use, tap where it shows up, and log from the home-screen or Lock Screen widget or with Siri without opening the app.

The app guides the trial step by step, with re-introduction windows based on published veterinary research and reminders about hidden diet breakers such as flavored medicines. Scanning a food bag flags ingredients the pet reacted to before. Patterns pool foods that share an ingredient, so a hidden trigger shows up across different treats. The vet report opens with a one-page summary. PawPlate does not diagnose; it turns an owner’s observations into a record the vet can use.

PawPlate is made by Mehmet Ali Kaplan, an independent developer in Türkiye, and is available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.
