🐶 🐱 For itchy dogs and cats

Find out what makes your pet itch.

Log the itch in 10 seconds a day. PawPlate guides the elimination diet, spots the foods behind flare-ups and hands your vet a one-page report. No sign-up needed.

Daily check-in

Ten seconds a day is enough.

Rate the itch from 0 to 10 on a scale modelled on the one vets use, tap where it shows up and note what your pet ate. That is the whole habit.

  • Itch score 0–10 and where on the body it shows up
  • Log from the home-screen or Lock Screen widget, or ask Siri
  • Add a photo or a short video of a flare-up
  • Gentle reminders at the time you pick
Elimination diet

An 8-week food trial, step by step.

Vets test for food allergy with one food for about eight weeks, then old foods back one at a time. PawPlate keeps the plan, the days and the rules for you.

  • Re-introduction windows based on published veterinary research
  • Warnings about hidden diet breakers such as flavored medicines
  • Scan a food bag to flag ingredients your pet reacted to before
  • Progress for each week of the trial
Patterns

The hidden trigger shows up.

Foods that share an ingredient are pooled, so chicken in a treat and chicken in a chew count together. Insights show which foods come before the bad days.

For the vet visit

A report your vet can read in 30 seconds.

Share a PDF that opens with a one-page summary — itch trend, diet and known reactions, current medicines — followed by the full log. PawPlate does not diagnose; it turns what you see at home into a record your vet can use.

  • One-page summary, then every check-in
  • A weekly summary of how it went
  • Weight, medicines and care in one place

Questions about PawPlate

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no email. If you want to restore your journal on another iPhone, you can turn on Sign in with Apple for the cloud backup.

Is it for dogs and cats?

Yes. You can add several pets, and the food guide rates every item separately for dogs and for cats.

Does PawPlate diagnose allergies?

No. It records what you observe and organises it so your vet can make the call. In an emergency, contact your vet or a poison hotline.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone. A daily backup to the cloud is on by default; you can switch it off and delete it in Settings. No ads and no tracking.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. PawPlate is made for iPhone and is on the App Store.

Which languages?

English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.

How do I cancel?

In your App Store account settings, at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.
