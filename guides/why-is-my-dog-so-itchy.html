Why is my dog so itchy?

Most itchy dogs have one of a handful of problems, and many have more than one at the same time. Where the itch shows up, and when it is worst, are the clues that help your vet sort them out faster.

Fleas and flea allergy

Some dogs are allergic to flea saliva, and a single bite can set off days of itch. The itch and sores sit mostly on the lower back, the base of the tail and the back of the thighs. Allergic dogs often chew the fleas away, so not finding any does not rule them out. Every pet in the house needs year-round prevention for it to work.

Environmental allergy (atopic dermatitis)

Pollen, house dust mites and molds cause the most common allergic skin disease in dogs. It usually starts between 6 months and 3 years of age. Dogs lick and chew their paws, rub their face, scratch their ears and armpits and lick the belly. At first it often follows the seasons, then can become year-round.

Food allergy

Food allergy itches in much the same places, but it tends to be there all year and does not follow the seasons. Repeated ear infections are common, and some dogs also have soft stools or go more often. In dogs the proteins most often involved are beef, dairy, chicken, wheat and lamb. The only dependable test is an elimination diet.

Infections and mites

Bacterial and yeast infections often move in on skin that is already inflamed, and they make the itch worse on their own: greasy skin, a musty smell, red-brown staining of the fur. Mites such as sarcoptic mange cause intense itch, often on the ear flaps, elbows and belly. These are the first things a vet checks, because they need their own treatment.

How your vet sorts it out

Itch in dogs is usually diagnosed step by step. Your vet first looks for parasites and infections and treats them, then makes sure flea control is strict, then runs an elimination diet. Environmental allergy is what remains when the others have been ruled out. Allergy tests for pollen and mites are used afterwards to choose immunotherapy, not to decide whether a dog is allergic.

What to note before the visit

  • Where: paws, ears, face, belly, armpits, lower back, tail base
  • When it is worst: at night, after walks, after meals, in which months
  • A daily itch score from 0 to 10, given the same way each day
  • Everything your dog eats, including treats and flavored medicines
  • When flea and worm prevention was last given, for every pet at home
  • Photos of the skin and a short video of a scratching episode

When to see a vet straight away

Go the same day if the face or muzzle swells, hives appear, the skin is bleeding or oozing, or your dog stops eating or seems unwell. Do not give human antihistamines, creams or other medicines without asking your vet first.

Frequently asked questions

Is it normal for a dog to scratch every day?

A little. In a study of more than 700 owners, healthy dogs mostly scored below 2 on a 0 to 10 itch scale. Scratching that wakes your dog up, interrupts meals or leaves marks is not normal.

Can dog food cause itching?

Yes. Food allergy is one of the main causes of itch in dogs, usually to a protein such as beef, dairy or chicken. An 8-week elimination diet is how vets check for it.
