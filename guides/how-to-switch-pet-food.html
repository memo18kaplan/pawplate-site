Switching foods without an upset stomach

Mix old and new over 7 to 10 days, and slow down if the stool gets soft.

2 min read

Go gradually

A sudden change can cause vomiting, diarrhea or a refused bowl. Over about a week, move from mostly old food to all new food, for example a quarter new for a couple of days, then half, then three quarters. Sensitive pets may need 10 days or more.

Watch and log

Log stool and appetite during the switch. If the stool turns soft, stay at the current mix a few extra days before moving on. Call your vet if vomiting or diarrhea keeps up.
